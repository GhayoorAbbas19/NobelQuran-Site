Privacy Policy — NobelQuran

Last updated: 2 October 2026 · ZX Apps

NobelQuran ("the app") is developed by ZX Apps. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have. Questions: sunnyg5525@gmail.com

Information we collect

Account. When you first open the app, an account with a random identifier is created automatically so your streak and community features work. No name, email or phone number is required. If you later create a full account, we store the details you choose to provide: display name, username, email address, phone number and an optional profile photo.

Reading activity. Your reading progress, daily goal, streak and estimated Hasanat are stored on your device. Your current streak and today's totals are also uploaded to our leaderboard service together with your display name.

We do not collect contacts, messages, browsing history or precise movement history. The app contains no advertising.

Crash reports and usage analytics

The app uses Google Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics. Crashlytics collects crash reports (stack traces, device model, operating system version) so we can find and fix bugs. Analytics collects aggregated usage statistics — such as which screens are opened and session counts — together with a device-scoped identifier. We use this data only to improve the app; it is not used for advertising and we do not sell it. This data is processed by Google on our behalf — see policies.google.com/privacy.

Location

If you grant location permission, your location is used only on your device to calculate local prayer times and the Qibla direction. Your location is never uploaded, stored on our servers or shared with anyone. You can use the rest of the app without granting location permission.

Leaderboard visibility

The community leaderboard shows your display name, optional profile photo, current streak and today's recorded reading to other users of the app. By default you appear under a neutral alias such as "Reader-4F2A"; you can change this name at any time in the Community screen. Private circles additionally show the same information to circle members. Your actual reading content and history are never shown to anyone.

Prayer Focus accessibility service

The optional Prayer Focus feature uses Android's AccessibilityService API, solely to recognise which app is in the foreground so that apps you chose to pause can be paused during your selected prayer windows. This processing happens entirely on your device. The service does not read the content of your screen, does not collect, store or transmit any personal or sensitive data, and is used for no purpose other than the app-pausing feature you configure. It is off by default and only runs after you enable it in Android's accessibility settings; you can turn it off there at any time.

Services we rely on

Firebase (Google LLC) provides sign-in, our database, photo storage, crash reporting and usage analytics. Data stored there is protected by security rules so each user can only write their own records. See Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Recitation audio is streamed from everyayah.com, and some translation text is fetched from alquran.cloud. As with any internet request, these services technically receive your IP address when the app downloads audio or text from them. We send them no account data.

Data retention and deletion

You can delete your account and its data from within the app: Settings → Profile & security → delete account. This removes your profile, leaderboard entries and uploaded photo from our systems. You can also email us at sunnyg5525@gmail.com to request deletion. Data stored only on your device is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

Security

All communication between the app and our services uses encrypted connections (HTTPS). Server-side security rules restrict every account to reading public leaderboard data and writing only its own records.

Children

NobelQuran is suitable for general audiences and shows no advertising. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond what is described above. If you believe a child has provided personal details without consent, contact us and we will delete them.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, the updated version will be shown here and inside the app with a new "last updated" date. Significant changes will be highlighted in the app.
