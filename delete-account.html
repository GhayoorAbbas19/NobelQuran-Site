Delete your NobelQuran account

NobelQuran · ZX Apps

This page explains how to delete your NobelQuran account and what happens to your data. It applies to the NobelQuran app by ZX Apps on Google Play (package com.zxapps.nobelquran).

Delete your account inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open NobelQuran
  2. Go to Settings (bottom navigation)
  3. Tap Profile & security
  4. Tap Delete account and confirm

Deletion takes effect immediately.

Or request deletion by email

Email sunnyg5525@gmail.com from any device. Include the email address, phone number or username linked to your NobelQuran account so we can find it. We process email deletion requests within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account (sign-in) and its identifier
  • Your profile: display name, username, email address, phone number and profile photo
  • Your leaderboard entries and circle membership

What is kept

  • No data that can identify you is kept after deletion.
  • Aggregated, anonymous statistics (such as crash reports and usage counts that are not linked to your identity) may be retained.
  • Reading progress stored only on your device is not on our servers; remove it by uninstalling the app or clearing the app's data in Android settings.

Questions

Contact sunnyg5525@gmail.com. See also our privacy policy.
